About Me

I am a performer based in Kuala Lumpur, driven by the raw energy of live theatre and choral music.

As a member of the Kuala Lumpur Men's Chorus (KLMC), I've had the privilege of bringing stories to life through music—from intimate ensemble pieces to full-scale musical productions. Whether I'm on stage as a character or blending my voice with the chorus, I believe in the power of performance to move, challenge, and connect.

I've performed in multiple productions of "Life Is...", KLMC's signature musical, working alongside talented artists and directors who have shaped my growth as a performer. Every show teaches me something new about vulnerability, presence, and the magic of live connection.

"From actuarial tables to centre stage."
